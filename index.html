The Problem

Traditional onboarding is broken

New employees must read dozens of pages of SOPs and policy documents with no guidance.

HR teams are constantly interrupted with repetitive questions, reducing their productivity.

Information shared verbally is inconsistent — different employees receive different answers.

The Solution

Onboarding Buddy System

Instant, accurate answers to onboarding questions — available 24/7, no waiting.

Grounded in official company documents — consistent and reliable every time.

Frees HR from repetitive queries so they can focus on strategic work.

Features

Everything a new hire needs

Instant Q&A

Ask anything about company policies, SOPs, tools, or culture and get an accurate answer in seconds.

Document Grounding

Answers are always based on official company documents — never hallucinated, always trustworthy.

24/7 Availability

Available at any time, any day — new hires don't need to wait for business hours to get help.

Friendly Persona

Buddy communicates in a warm, approachable tone — making new hires feel welcomed, not overwhelmed.

Smart Escalation

When Buddy doesn't know the answer, it gracefully redirects to the right HR contact — no dead ends.

Powered by IBM Bob

Built on IBM Bob's powerful AI Agent framework — enterprise-grade, reliable, and easy to maintain.

How It Works

1

New hire asks a question

3
